About Me

I am an undergraduate student majoring in Physics at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU). But my interests lie at analog application-specific integrated circuits (ASICs), embedded systems, and high-precision signal read-out systems.

I currently work in the Advanced Electronics of Extreme Environment (AEEE) Lab at SJTU’s School of Physics, where I explore electronic systems for demanding measurement environments. I will start my Ph.D. studies in 2027 and continue focusing on the analog integrated circuit design.
